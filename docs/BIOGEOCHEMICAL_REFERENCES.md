# Sources for the biogeochemical rule panel

The keys below resolve literature sources retained in the curated category and rule files. They document the preceding research snapshot, not an assertion that each tool was executed. KEGG expressions are retained as selected frozen analysis definitions.

- **KEGG:** [module syntax and definitions](https://www.kegg.jp/kegg/module.html); individual module URLs are recorded in the configuration.

- **NCycDB:** Tu, Qichao et al. NCycDB: a curated integrative database for fast and accurate metagenomic profiling of nitrogen cycling genes. DOI: [10.1093/bioinformatics/bty741](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/bty741).
- **SCycDB:** Yu, Xiaoli et al. SCycDB: A curated functional gene database for metagenomic profiling of sulphur cycling pathways. DOI: [10.1111/1755-0998.13306](https://doi.org/10.1111/1755-0998.13306).
- **MCycDB:** Qian, Lu et al. MCycDB: A curated database for comprehensively profiling methane cycling processes of environmental microbiomes. DOI: [10.1111/1755-0998.13589](https://doi.org/10.1111/1755-0998.13589).
- **PCycDB:** Zeng, Jiaxiong et al. PCycDB: a comprehensive and accurate database for fast analysis of phosphorus cycling genes. DOI: [10.1186/s40168-022-01292-1](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40168-022-01292-1).
- **SMDB:** Mo, Shuming et al. Sulfur metabolism in subtropical marine mangrove sediments fundamentally differs from other habitats as revealed by SMDB. DOI: [10.1038/s41598-023-34995-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-34995-y).
- **METABOLIC:** Zhou, Zhichao et al. METABOLIC: high-throughput profiling of microbial genomes for functional traits, metabolism, biogeochemistry, and community-scale functional networks. DOI: [10.1186/s40168-021-01213-8](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40168-021-01213-8).
- **DiTing:** Xue, Chun-Xu et al. DiTing: A Pipeline to Infer and Compare Biogeochemical Pathways From Metagenomic and Metatranscriptomic Data. DOI: [10.3389/fmicb.2021.698286](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2021.698286).
- **FeGenie:** Garber, Arkadiy I. et al. FeGenie: A Comprehensive Tool for the Identification of Iron Genes and Iron Gene Neighborhoods in Genome and Metagenome Assemblies. DOI: [10.3389/fmicb.2020.00037](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2020.00037).
- **CANTHYD:** Khot, Varada et al. CANT-HYD: A Curated Database of Phylogeny-Derived Hidden Markov Models for Annotation of Marker Genes Involved in Hydrocarbon Degradation. DOI: [10.3389/fmicb.2021.764058](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2021.764058).
- **HydDB:** Søndergaard, Dan et al. HydDB: A web tool for hydrogenase classification and analysis. DOI: [10.1038/srep34212](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep34212).
- **dbCAN3:** Zheng, Jinfang et al. dbCAN3: automated carbohydrate-active enzyme and substrate annotation. DOI: [10.1093/nar/gkad328](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkad328).
- **CCycDB:** Zhou, Jiayin et al. CCycDB: an integrative knowledgebase to fingerprint microbially mediated carbon cycling processes. DOI: [10.64898/2026.01.28.702190](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.01.28.702190). **Preprint in this snapshot; not treated as peer-reviewed validation.**
- **Nif2012:** Dos Santos, Patricia C et al. Distribution of nitrogen fixation and nitrogenase-like sequences amongst microbial genomes. DOI: [10.1186/1471-2164-13-162](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2164-13-162).
- **Anammox2011:** Kartal, Boran et al. Molecular mechanism of anaerobic ammonium oxidation. DOI: [10.1038/nature10453](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10453).
- **Comammox2015a:** van Kessel, Maartje A. H. J. et al. Complete nitrification by a single microorganism. DOI: [10.1038/nature16459](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature16459).
- **Comammox2015b:** Daims, Holger et al. Complete nitrification by Nitrospira bacteria. DOI: [10.1038/nature16461](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature16461).
- **Dsr2018:** Anantharaman, Karthik et al. Expanded diversity of microbial groups that shape the dissimilatory sulfur cycle. DOI: [10.1038/s41396-018-0078-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41396-018-0078-0).
- **AlkylCoM2016:** Laso-Pérez, Rafael et al. Thermophilic archaea activate butane via alkyl-coenzyme M formation. DOI: [10.1038/nature20152](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature20152).
- **rGly2020:** Sánchez-Andrea, Irene et al. The reductive glycine pathway allows autotrophic growth of Desulfovibrio desulfuricans. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-020-18906-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-18906-7).
- **roTCA2021:** Steffens, Lydia et al. High CO2 levels drive the TCA cycle backwards towards autotrophy. DOI: [10.1038/s41586-021-03456-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03456-9).
- **roTCA2024:** Gao, Lei et al. Reversed oxidative TCA (roTCA) for carbon fixation by an Acidimicrobiia strain from a saline lake. DOI: [10.1093/ismejo/wrae147](https://doi.org/10.1093/ismejo/wrae147).
- **Mangrove2023:** Qian, Lu et al. Vertically stratified methane, nitrogen and sulphur cycling and coupling mechanisms in mangrove sediment microbiomes. DOI: [10.1186/s40168-023-01501-5](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40168-023-01501-5).
- **rbims2026:** López-Martínez, Karla P. et al. rbims: an R package for integrative functional profiling and pathway-level discrimination in metagenome-assembled genomes. DOI: [10.3389/fbinf.2026.1831383](https://doi.org/10.3389/fbinf.2026.1831383).

Additional references for the interpretation of the C–P lyase machinery and nitrogenase variants:

- [Structural/mechanistic study of the C–P lyase complex](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-36604-y).
- [Nitrogenase evolution and variants](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msae023). The expanded NifHDKENB rule is an additional conservative criterion, not a universal requirement.

Sequence-specialist tools such as FeGenie, dbCAN3, HydDB and CANT-HYD identify work requiring new evidence. Their inclusion here does not transform generic KO annotations into specialist classifications. METABOLIC is a methodological reference; this workflow is a separately specified KO-rule evaluator.
